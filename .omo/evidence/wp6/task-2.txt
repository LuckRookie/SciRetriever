Schema/transaction assertions
- diagnostic_records supports pre-identity input_fingerprint without a Work row and explicit
  Work, WorkVersion and processing-run FKs for catalog subjects.
- stage, subject, reason and action are closed CHECK vocabularies; retryable is boolean; summary,
  canonical details JSON and UTF-8 byte size are bounded; rows are UPDATE/DELETE protected.
- curation_operations stores action/subject, canonical bounded before/after/evidence JSON,
  before/after/stale-guard SHA-256, RFC3339 time and unique self-FK undo_of_operation_id.
  Action/subject combinations are checked and reverse lookup supplies the undone-by link.
- Work and Author lineage retain source tombstones, target and operation FKs, reject self merge,
  reject recursive cycles, are indexed by target and are append-only.
- invalid subject, unknown action, 16385-byte snapshot, missing FK, duplicate undo, append-only
  mutation and cyclic Work merge all reject. The cyclic operation+lineage transaction rolls back;
  the prior lineage count remains exactly 1.
